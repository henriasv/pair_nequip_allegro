Scaling analysis

How multi-GPU pair_nequip scales

The complete measured picture, 1–64 GCDs: strong scaling, weak scaling, the per-step cost model behind both, per-GCD capacity, and the comparison against the ML-IAP route. LUMI-G MI250X, OAM-M fine-tuned model, OpenEquivariance kernels, fp32 inference, CPU-bound runs throughout (June 2026 campaign + the August 64-GCD extension).

Three findings carry everything else:

1. Throughput is set almost entirely by atoms per GCD — one master curve.

2. The curve flattens at a fixed per-step floor (~13 ms MgO+water, ~10 ms Cu): small cells stop gaining at 8–16 GCDs, but that is launch/exchange latency, not a communication wall — no ceiling was found at 64 GCDs (8 nodes, the most tested).

3. Keep ≳1 000 atoms/GCD and both strong (84%/doubling) and weak (~flat) scaling stay healthy.

1One chart: throughput vs atoms per GCD

Every MgO+water measurement from every series — strong, weak, 1 to 64 GCDs, cells of 360 to 184 320 atoms — plotted against the atoms each GCD owns. They collapse onto one curve; only the single-GCD points (no inter-rank exchange at all) sit slightly above it.

2Strong scaling — same cell, more GPUs

Solid lines are measurements; the dashed gray line is ideal scaling from the 1-GCD point. The ML-IAP route runs the identical model and OEQ kernels, so the gap is the coupling overhead.

Small cells (the active-learning regime)

pair_nequip saturates ~2.2–2.6× higher than mliap/kk (77 vs 36; 98 vs 38 ts/s). Saturation is the per-step floor: at 32 GCDs these cells leave only 90–64 atoms per GCD.

Large cells (the production regime)

The 23 040-atom pair_nequip series extends to 64 GCDs (measured 2026-08-19; the same-allocation 32-GCD rerun gave 44.8 vs June's 45.0). mliap was not re-run at 64; its 32-GCD trend had already flattened (§C.7 of the wiki guide records its multi-node variance separately).

3Weak scaling — grow the cell with the GPU count

Constant atoms/GCD; flat is perfect. The win per doubling is capacity: twice the atoms simulated at the same pace.

At ≳1 400 atoms/GCD, strong scaling continues essentially unimpeded through 8 nodes; at 360 atoms/GCD gains shrink toward the floor but have not stopped. Nothing in the 32→64 step suggests an interconnect wall — weak scaling is flat, and throughput at fixed atoms/GCD is the same whether the ranks span 4 nodes or 8 (58.3 ts/s for 11 520 atoms on 32 GCDs vs 59.5 for 23 040 on 64, both 360 atoms/GCD). Beyond 64 GCDs is untested.

5Per-GCD capacity (why OEQ is the default)

One 64 GB GCD, fp32 OAM-M inference, ramping atoms until out-of-memory. OpenEquivariance's streamed tensor-product contractions never materialize the large per-edge intermediates: ~4× the capacity (46–92k vs 11.5–23k atoms) and ~10× the speed at equal size.

6The cost model, and how to size a run

Per step, per GCD, the data fit a two-term model:

t_step ≈ t_floor + c · (atoms/GCD)

with t_floor ≈ 13 ms (MgO+water; ~10 ms for Cu) — the per-layer exchange latency plus kernel-launch overhead, independent of size — and c ≈ 16–18 µs·GCD/atom for OAM-M with OEQ on MI250X. This one equation reproduces the whole family of curves above: strong scaling bends over when c·atoms/GCD shrinks toward t_floor (≈800 atoms/GCD for the halfway point), weak scaling is flat because atoms/GCD is constant, and the collapse plot in §1 is the model.

Sizing a run: pick N_GCD ≈ atoms / 1000–3000. The lower end buys speed at ~80% efficiency; the upper end is nearly free of overhead. Below ~400 atoms/GCD you are paying full nodes for the floor. Small cells (≲3 000 atoms) top out at 8–16 GCDs — beyond that, spend the GPUs on more replicas instead.

7Methodology & provenance

All numbers are bound runs (LUMI CCD↔GCD adjacency masks; --cpu-bind=none inflates multi-node variance catastrophically — up to 57% of loop time in sync-wait — and was the June campaign's headline methodology correction). Both engines were compiled from the same fine-tuned .nequip.zip on the deployed module, with OEQ on both. Environments, gate results, and the engine comparison in full detail: the evaluation page §6. June series: campaign report pair_nequip_vs_mliap_bound_20260628.md; 64-GCD extension: LUMI job 21346291 (2026-08-19) with same-allocation 32-GCD baselines. Every chart's data is in its "data table" fold-out.
